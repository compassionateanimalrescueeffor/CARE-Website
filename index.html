Our Mission

Every Rescue Starts With Hope

Every year, thousands of animals are left abandoned, sick, or injured without access to safety or proper care. At Compassionate Animal Rescue Effort (CARE), we believe every life is worth fighting for.

This campaign will fund the first phase of establishing a future animal rescue shelter by purchasing essential equipment needed to care for rescued animals. By securing critical crates, medical stations, clean bedding, and rescue equipment, we ensure our emergency team can begin rescuing abandoned and injured animals.

Strategic Plan

Campaign Roadmap

Our structured 3-phase plan to build, equip, and operate a safe haven for animals in need.

Phase 1 • Active Now

Launch & Essential Equipment

Goal $3,500

Purchasing core rescue gear, cages, medical kits, and hygiene supplies to initiate rescue operations.

  • Rescue crates & transport carriers
  • Veterinary first-aid kits
  • Food & sanitization stations
Phase 2 • Upcoming

Facility Preparation

Goal $25,000

Leasing physical shelter premises, securing indoor/outdoor play runs, and installing climate control systems.

  • Shelter building lease & renovation
  • Dog play yard fencing & artificial turf
  • Isolation rooms for sick animals
Phase 3 • Future Goal

Shelter Operations

Goal $100,000+

Hiring full-time care staff, launching mobile adoption clinics, and expanding regional rescue coverage.

  • On-site veterinary clinic setup
  • 24/7 animal emergency rescue vehicle
  • Community spay & neuter outreach
